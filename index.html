Privacy

financer is a personal finance app. It runs on a server its owner runs for themself, and it is used only by that owner. Nobody else's data is processed.

What financer reads

  • Through Enable Banking (account information, read-only): the accounts the owner chooses to share, their balances and their booked transactions.
  • Nothing else from the bank. financer can't make payments, transfers or other orders.

Where it is kept

  • On the owner's own financer server, in its database. The Enable Banking session is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • financer never sees or stores the online banking login, PIN or TAN for Enable Banking connections: the owner confirms access on the bank's own pages.

Who else gets it

  • No one. financer has no analytics, tracking, advertising or other third parties, and doesn't sell or share data.
  • Enable Banking Oy relays the data from the bank under its own privacy notice, at enablebanking.com.

How long, and how to stop it

  • Access lasts as long as the bank's consent (usually 180 days) and ends sooner when the owner removes the connection in financer, which also closes the session at Enable Banking.
  • The consent can be withdrawn at any time at the bank as well.
  • Removing an account in financer deletes its balances and transactions from the server.

Terms

  • financer is for its owner's personal, non-commercial use. It isn't offered to other people.
  • It only reads account information. It never initiates payments.
  • Balances, projections and categories are estimates from the data the bank provides. They are not financial advice, and the bank's own statements are what count.
  • financer comes without warranty. Its owner runs it at their own risk and keeps the server and its access token safe.
  • Use of the bank connection is also subject to the bank's terms and Enable Banking's terms.
